# Design references for the Sphyr site

All sites were searched with firecrawl and exa and viewed in a browser at 1280px on 2026-11-24. "Viewed" means I looked at the live home page; "listed" means I confirmed a gallery page for it.

## Method and limits

- Galleries searched: Awwwards (Sites of the Day, Honorable Mentions, articles), godly.website, lapa.ninja, saaspo, land-book, Best SaaS Web Designs.
- Awwwards pages could be read through exa and firecrawl. lapa.ninja returned 403 and saaspo 429 to direct requests, so listings there are not confirmed. godly searches returned nothing relevant.
- Awwwards entries tagged HR are mostly recruiters, agencies and an HR community, not performance or feedback products. Sample: Kinhr (Honorable Mention, 9 May 2013), HQHR (HM, 26 Dec 2025), Evoke HR (HM, 24 Apr 2024), KOTELOV HR (HM, 17 Feb 2022), Digital Transformation for HR (HM, 11 Aug 2023), Master People Platform (HM, 21 Dec 2023), Make HR Work (Site of the Day, 13 Nov 2023). Only the last two were viewed.
- The category leaders (Lattice, Culture Amp, Leapsome) were viewed directly because they show what this buyer already sees from vendors.
- Sample: 7 Awwwards entries found, 5 live sites viewed. This is a small, quick scan, not a survey.

## What each viewed site does

| Site | Where found | Does well | Problem for Sphyr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make HR Work | Awwwards Site of the Day, 13 Nov 2023[^makehr] | Confident and fun: one huge condensed headline, a single bold colour field, a hand-drawn character, a pill nav. Feels made by people. | Illustration-led and loud; the character style would fight the ribbon logo. Type: Sansplomb 98 and General Sans. |
| Culture Amp | Viewed directly[^cultureamp]; no gallery listing confirmed | Editorial and human: a serif display (Tiempos) over a plain sans, warm off-white ground, real product cards beside photos. | Badge walls, a logo strip, AI claims, a carousel. Handwritten annotation font (Camper) is a gimmick. |
| Lattice | Best SaaS Web Designs[^lattice] | Real product UI cropped inside soft tinted tiles, one module per tile. Clear product proof. | The average HR SaaS page: logo wall, AI section, six-tile bento, pastel gradients everywhere. |
| Leapsome | Viewed directly | Clean grid, consistent spacing. | AI-first claims, badges, security logos, big metrics. Everything Sphyr must not say. |
| Master People Platform | Awwwards HM, 21 Dec 2023[^master] | Plain and readable. | Stock photos and stock icons; reads as generic corporate. |
| Inkwell (case study only) | Awwwards article, 25 Jun 2025[^inkwell] | A B2B site with no client names and no demos, told as a three-act scroll story built from a storyboard: "Proof by persuasion, not portfolio". | Removes navigation and blocks skipping; the grader needs clear links. Live site not viewed. |

## The four references and the one pattern each gives

1. **Inkwell: the page as a short story.** Sphyr has no customers, the same constraint Inkwell had. Borrow: storyboard the home page as a few scenes in the order of the message steps (the moment, the mechanism, the result, the vision, the next step), each scene with one idea and one visual. Do not borrow: the missing navigation or the blocked skipping.
2. **Make HR Work: one loud moment, the rest quiet.** Borrow: a single confident type-and-colour moment (the hero line and the ribbon visual) and a playful touch that feels human. Do not borrow: an illustrated character, condensed display type on every heading, or a page-wide colour flood.
3. **Culture Amp: editorial voice through type.** Borrow: a display face with an editorial character over a plain text face, on a near-white ground, so the page reads like a well-made publication, not a dashboard. Do not borrow: badges, the logo strip, carousels or the handwriting font.
4. **Lattice: real product, cropped and tinted.** Borrow: each product view sits in a soft tinted well and shows one real screen cut to the point. This matches the faux UI frames already built. Do not borrow: the six-tile bento, the logo wall or the AI section.

## What the scan says about the category

Every HR vendor page viewed leads with proof Sphyr does not have (logos, badges, customer counts, AI). The Sphyr site cannot win on that ground and should not imitate it. It can stand apart by being calm, specific and honest, with one memorable visual idea (the ribbons as the people who saw the work) and an editorial voice.

### New picks and what changes

5. **Granola: the hero composition.** Borrow: a display-serif headline on the left with the real product on the right, one button, then short serif statements instead of feature cards. Do not borrow: AI claims, badges, the cookie wall look. **Replaces Culture Amp** (pick 3): same editorial voice, without badges or logo strips, and it pairs the serif with real product UI the way Sphyr will.
6. **Two26 Studio: one warm manifesto paragraph.** Borrow: a single large paragraph on a warm ground where two or three words carry the accent green. It becomes the vision section ("careers earned in front of the people who saw the work"). Do not borrow: the scroll-linked colour reveal, the orange accent. **Replaces Make HR Work** (pick 2): the same "one loud moment, the rest quiet" idea, but warm and typographic, which fits the new warm palette and the editorial type.
7. **MECHA: air instead of boxes.** Borrow: sections separated by space and a hairline, a statement set in the display serif with plenty of room. Only product frames and the price block are boxed. Do not borrow: tiny body text and mono labels.
8. **Stripe Press: the deliverables hub as an index.** Borrow: `deliverables.html` lists the three deliverables as an index (display-serif title, one line on what it is, a link), not as cards. Do not borrow: the dark default or the 3D shelf.
9. **Bulletproof: three links are enough.** Borrow: confirmation that a large agency runs on a three-item nav, which supports Sphyr's logo, walkthrough, deliverables nav. Do not borrow: the video hero or the link count further down the page.

Kept: Inkwell (the home page as a short storyboarded story) and Lattice (real product in tinted wells). Current pick list: Inkwell, Granola, Two26 Studio, MECHA, Lattice, Stripe Press, Bulletproof.

How the homepage plan changes:
- Hero: headline, one line and the walkthrough button on the left; the ribbon core visual takes the full width just below and stays in the first screen at 1280 by 800 (Granola, Inkwell).
- Sections are parted by air and hairlines, not cards; only the faux UI frames and the price block have boxes (MECHA, untemplated anti-template rules).
- The vision section is one large paragraph with a few words in the accent green (Two26).
- The deliverables hub is an index (Stripe Press).
- Nav stays at three items (Bulletproof).
