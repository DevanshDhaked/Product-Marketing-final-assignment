## 2.2 Pilot setup (real data point, self-reported) (WP p3)
- "For the past three months, we implemented a manual version (no software) of our methodology in four different companies."
- Companies: two startups (24 and 43 employees), a "small company of 200+ employees" (sample 51), a "relatively large organization with over 5000 employees" (sample "70+").
- Fortnightly loop: "members would receive and give feedback every alternate week."
- Important: the pilot was "paper and pen" (WP p4). It validated the methodology, not the software. Do not say "Sphyr software pilot".

## 2.3 Pilot results (WP p4, self-reported)
1. "The resistance to provide feedback fell by 34% over the period of 6 weeks and to 88% by the end of the 14-week trial" (wording is ambiguous: "to 88%"; also 14 weeks vs "three months"; quote exactly or avoid).
2. "The tendency to collude for better reviews/ratings dropped to 0% after just 3 weeks".
3. "The average time to fill up the feedback cards (for an average sphere size of 14 members) was a little over two minutes (02:09 mins)". Safest headline stat.
4. "The average acceptance rate of fairness of reviews increased by 57% to approximately 94%".
5. "The frequency of appeal ... fell by 92% and the duration of discussion on appeal dropped from an average of 25 minutes to 3.5 minutes".
6. HR effort to collate and analyze "dropped sharply by 76%" (collation was done manually by the Sphyr team to simulate software).
7. "Employee Satisfaction ... increased by 53% in the same period."

## 2.4 Cost case (WP p4-p5)
- Baseline survey: self review form 60 min; per subordinate 50 min; 1000 manager minutes for 20 subordinates; appeals 40%; mediation 45 min; 3 HR follow-ups; 11 min to finalize per employee; rates US$26/h employee, US$45/h manager, US$21/h HR.
- "For a company with a headcount of 100 members, the total cost of appraisal per annum was calculated at ... US$ 8,500, or US$ 85 per employee." All review types estimated "about $17,000 per annum for 100 members" (rough estimate from HR conversations).
- With Sphyr method: 132 min per employee per annum; 0 manager minutes (manager is "just another employee"); appeals 3.2%; 3 min appeal discussion; 0.2 HR follow-ups; 0.5 min to generate reports.
- Cost table: existing US$8,500 (US$85/employee, excludes software) vs Sphyr US$6,258 (US$63/employee, "includes the cost of the software and other forms of reviews"). This is a modeled estimate; the 2018 Sphyr software price behind it is not stated.
