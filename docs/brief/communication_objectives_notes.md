## What this means for the final assignment

- The final brief asks for one propaganda strategy and one advertisement. In this framework they are different objectives: propaganda changes a belief, advertising is a paid push.
- An "advertisement" here implies paid placement. A free poster or a post is word of mouth, PR or content marketing, not advertising. So the one ad should be a paid placement, kept small and non-invasive to stay consistent with the zero-budget, no-tracking channel plan.
- The three LinkedIn posts are content marketing (pull) and, for post 1, propaganda. The website serves educational and informational objectives.
