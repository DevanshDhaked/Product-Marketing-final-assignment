# Pricing summary for writers

**Status: proposal. Do not put any of this on the site, in a post or in the ad until the team approves the numbers.** The reasoning is in [price and pilot](price_and_pilot.md).

## The price

**₹199 per employee per month, billed yearly, plus GST.** The same price for every employee and every firm size. A 150-person firm pays ₹29,850 a month, ₹3,58,200 a year, plus GST.

## What it includes

- A two-minute feedback card every two weeks, for the colleagues each person worked with.
- One record per person built from those cards, with ratings pooled and comments anonymized to the person rated.
- The firm's own values on the cards, and team and company OKRs.
- Each person's own view of their record, and manager views of team and member trends.
- Import of the people list from a spreadsheet. Clients and other outside raters invited by email, at no charge.
- Setup, training and support.
- New joiners billed from the month they join.

## The pilot

**12 weeks, six cycles, one or two practices of up to 40 people, ₹25,000 plus GST.**

- It ends before the April appraisal round if it starts by 7 January 2027.
- If the firm signs within 60 days of the last cycle, the whole ₹25,000 comes off the first annual bill.
- If it does not, the fee is refunded in full when the firm took part properly (80% or more of people in at least 5 of the 6 cycles) and fewer than half of its partners say the record showed them something they did not know. Otherwise the fee is kept.
- Either way, we send the firm all its data as spreadsheets within 7 days of asking, and delete its account and data within 30 days after the 60-day read-only period, confirmed in writing.
- Before the pilot starts, the firm and Sphyr agree in writing what it must show.

## Three numbers that justify the price

1. **One consultant who stays covers more than two years of Sphyr.** By our middle estimate, losing one consultant costs a firm about ₹8.6 L in fees, empty-seat time and ramp-up. Sphyr for 150 people costs ₹3.58 L a year. This is our estimate of exit cost, not a measured Sphyr result.
2. **Less than half the price of a dedicated review tool.** ₹199 is 43% of the lowest list price we found for one (PerformYard's $5, about ₹464 a seat, for firms of several hundred people), and about a quarter of Peoplebox's and Confirm's starting price (about ₹743). Prices read 24 November 2026; check them again before publishing.
3. **The pilot fee is our cost, not a profit.** ₹25,000 is what 12 weeks of setup, training, support and the results review cost us, and all of it counts towards the first year.

## What not to say

- Not "anonymous to everyone". Managers who approve edit requests can see who gave feedback to whom. Say "ratings are pooled and comments are anonymized to the person rated".
- No AI, no integrations, no mobile apps, no security or compliance certificates.
- No rupee saving as a Sphyr result, and no "customers save" or "firms see". There are no customers yet.
- Not "cheaper than Keka". It is not: Keka's whole HR system costs about ₹157 a seat at 150 people. Sphyr sits next to the HR system; it does not replace it.
- Not "money back, no questions asked". The refund has two conditions, stated above.
