# Pricing summary for writers

**Status: decided by the team at gate 1b.** Use these terms exactly as written. The reasoning is in [price and pilot](price_and_pilot.md).

## The price

**₹149 per employee per month, billed yearly, plus GST.** The same price for every employee and every firm size. A 150-person firm pays ₹22,350 a month, ₹2,68,200 a year, plus GST.

## What it includes

- A two-minute feedback card every two weeks, for the colleagues each person worked with.
- One record per person built from those cards, with ratings pooled and comments anonymized to the person rated.
- The firm's own values on the cards, and team and company OKRs.
- Each person's own view of their record, and manager views of team and member trends.
- Import of the people list from a spreadsheet. Clients and other outside raters invited by email, at no charge.
- Setup, training and support.
- New joiners billed from the month they join.

## The pilot

**12 weeks, six cycles, up to 40 people, ₹25,000 plus GST.**

- If the firm signs, the ₹25,000 comes off its first annual bill.
- If it does not, it gets its data as a spreadsheet (CSV) and the rest is deleted. The fee is not refunded.

## Three numbers that justify the price

1. **One consultant who stays covers about three years of Sphyr.** By our middle estimate, losing one consultant costs a firm about ₹8.6 L in recruiter fees, empty-seat time and ramp-up. Sphyr for 150 people costs ₹2.68 L a year. This is our estimate of what an exit costs, not a measured Sphyr result.
2. **About a third of the price of a dedicated review tool.** ₹149 is 32% of the lowest list price we found for one (PerformYard's $5, about ₹464 a seat, for firms of several hundred people), and about a fifth of Peoplebox's and Confirm's starting price (about ₹743). Prices read 24 November 2026; check them again before publishing.
3. **The pilot fee is our cost, not a profit.** ₹25,000 is what 12 weeks of setup, training, support and the results review cost us, and all of it counts towards the first year if the firm signs.

## What not to say

- Not "anonymous to everyone". Managers who approve edit requests can see who gave feedback to whom. Say "ratings are pooled and comments are anonymized to the person rated".
- No AI, no integrations, no mobile apps, no security or compliance certificates.
- No rupee saving as a Sphyr result, and no "customers save" or "firms see". There are no customers yet.
- No refund, money-back or price-lock promise. None was adopted.
- Do not name Keka or other HR systems in public copy. If a buyer asks, Sphyr sits next to the HR system; it does not replace it.
