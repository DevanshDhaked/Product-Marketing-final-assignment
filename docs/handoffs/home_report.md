The managing partner has asked you to make this year go better than the last, and this is all you have to send."

Four scenes (labelled rows, h3 label plus text):
1. "The one who presented gets the credit." The consultant who walked the client through the findings gets the credit, including for the model a colleague built the night before.
2. "The last engagement counts for the most." Whoever rates a consultant mostly remembers the latest engagement. The three before it had other managers, and those managers are busy on new clients.
3. "The forms never come back." Engagement managers move straight to the next client, and some never fill in the project-end form. One has left the firm, so their forms will never arrive.
4. "A quiet consultant leaves." A strong consultant who rarely presents is passed over and resigns. In the exit interview they say the partner who rated them saw one engagement out of four.
