## 2.8 Pricing (graded 70/100, `Sphyr_Pricing_OnePager.pdf`, 2 Nov)
Every number, verbatim where quoted:
- Header: "Continuous 360° feedback for 50–300-person companies in India · November 2026".
- Price: "₹149 per employee per month. Billed yearly, plus GST. The same price for every employee and every company size."
- Strategy / model / rule: "Value-based pricing | One flat per-employee price | No tiers, no limits, no price jumps".
- Included: "Feedback cards and dashboards; OKRs and AI feedback summaries; Setup, training and support; Clients and vendors who give feedback: free; New joiners billed from the month they join".
- Example bills per month: 100 employees ₹14,900; 101 (one new hire) ₹15,049 (+₹149); 150 ₹22,350; 300 ₹44,700. [arithmetic checked: correct]
- COST: "₹30 To serve one employee a month: hosting, AI, support, payments. ₹30 ÷ (1 − 80% margin) = ₹150 floor".
- VALUE: "₹667 Per employee a month: one fewer resignation a year (₹500) + less review prep (₹167). Customer keeps 78% of the value". [check: (667 − 149) / 667 = 77.7%, correct]
- MARKET: "₹665–1,425 Per employee a month for Leapsome, Lattice and Peoplebox. Sphyr is one-fifth or less". [check: ₹665 / ₹95 = $7, ₹1,425 / ₹95 = $15. 149 / 665 = 22.4%, so "one-fifth or less" is false at the low end of the range; true only against ₹745 or more. No plan names given.]
- 150 vs 149: the PDF shows ₹150 as the "floor" and recommends ₹149 with no stated reason (feedback point). Note: ₹149 is below the stated ₹150 floor, so it implies margin of (149 − 30)/149 = 79.9%, just under 80%. [my arithmetic]
- Break-even: "₹119 earned per employee after costs. ₹8 L fixed costs a month ÷ ₹119 ≈ 6,700 employees, about 67 companies of 100". [check: 149 − 30 = 119; 800,000 / 119 = 6,723. Correct.] No fixed-cost breakdown anywhere in the repo.
- Pilot: "6-week pilot, one team of up to 25: ₹15,000, adjusted against the first yearly bill". [my arithmetic: 6 weeks is about 1.38 months; ₹15,000 / (25 x 1.38) is about ₹435 per employee-month, about 2.9x list; at list price 25 x 1.38 x 149 is about ₹5,140. 6 weeks = 3 fortnightly cycles.] No basis, no non-conversion terms stated.
- Lock-in: "First 25 customers: price locked for 2 years in exchange for a case study".
- Testing: "Van Westendorp price survey with 40–60 HR buyers, pilot-to-paid conversion, and a quarterly margin review".
- Footnote: "Competitor list prices read 2 Nov 2026 at $1 ≈ ₹95. Costs and value are team estimates. Method: PMA, How to price a product; Hinterhuber (2008); Van Westendorp (1976)."
- What is NOT in the PDF or anywhere in repo: how ₹500 attrition value is derived (the implied assumption, my inference: a 100-person company avoids one resignation a year worth ₹6 L, since 6,00,000 / 100 / 12 = ₹500); how ₹167 review prep is derived (₹2,000 per employee a year, no basis found); breakdown of ₹30 cost; breakdown of ₹8 L fixed cost; whether ₹30 includes onboarding/setup labour (setup, training and support are "Included" in the price, so their cost should sit in variable or fixed cost).
