How it works

Many honest views, pooled over time.

Sphyr adds one small habit to your company: two minutes, every fortnight, rating the people you actually worked with. Here's what happens to those ratings.

1 · The Sphere

Everyone who sees the work gets a say

Each employee's Sphere is the set of people they actually work with: their manager, peers, juniors, other team leads, and people outside the company such as clients and vendors.

Clients and vendors who give feedback never cost you anything.

2 · The card

Five clicks per person

A rating

How was working with this person this fortnight?

What stood out

Tags drawn from your company's and team's own values.

An optional note

Anonymous, delayed and released in batches.

Behind the tags, Sphyr tracks seven parameters: professional behaviour, timeliness of delivery, quality of work, adherence to policies, resolving dependencies, teamwork and engagement.

In the 2018 pen-and-paper pilot of the method, a fortnight's cards for a Sphere of about 14 people took around 2 minutes 9 seconds.

3 · Pooling and anonymity

Nobody sees who wrote what. Not the manager, not HR.

Pooled first

Ratings are combined and anonymized before anyone sees them.

Minimum reviewers

Results appear only after enough reviewers have responded, so no one rating stands out.

Delayed notes

Written notes can be held and released in batches, so timing doesn't give anyone away.

4 · Values cascade

Your values become the questions

Leadership sets company values

Founders or HR choose the values that apply to everyone.

Managers add team values

Each team adds what matters for its own work.

They appear as tags

Every card asks about the behaviour your company says it rewards.

5 · Decisions stay human

Managers still decide. Now they have evidence.

Sphyr doesn't calculate promotions. At appraisal time, managers and founders see months of pooled ratings across the seven parameters, from everyone around each person. The decision is still theirs.

Runs alongside your HR system

Sphyr doesn't replace your HRMS or payroll. It's a feedback layer next to it.
