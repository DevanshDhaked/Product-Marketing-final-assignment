Two minutes every two weeks

Consultants move from one engagement to the next, and the people who saw their best work move on too. So Sphyr asks while the team is still together.

Every two weeks, each person gets a short card for everyone they worked with, and the whole set takes about two minutes. They choose how working with that person went and what stood out. A note is optional.

What Sphyr does not do

Sphyr hands partners a record and leaves the decision with them. The feedback in that record comes only from what colleagues chose to say on their cards, and Sphyr logs no hours or screen activity. No AI writes or scores anything. Your people come in from a spreadsheet, and Sphyr sits beside your HR system.

What stays private

Ratings are pooled, and the person rated sees comments without names.

Who sees what I write?
The person you wrote about sees your note without your name, and ratings are pooled before anyone looks at them. Managers approve requests to edit feedback that has already been sent, so they can see who asked to edit feedback about whom.
What if I didn't work with someone this fortnight?
Choose "No interaction" and move on.
Can clients rate me?
Only if you invite them, with your partner's agreement. Clients can join from the second cycle.
What if I miss a cycle?
The next one opens two weeks later. Your Insights page notes the missed cycle, and that's all.

Try it with up to 40 people first

Every pilot runs against the same scorecard, and your firm agrees the targets before it starts.

What every pilot measures

  • How many people take part in each cycle.
  • How many different people give feedback to each consultant.
  • How many senior consultants have feedback from people on three or more engagements by calibration.
  • Whether partners found something new in the records, and whether it changed any discussion.
  • Whether consultants found their own record useful, and whether they would keep going.

We publish results only with the firm's consent, and without its name unless it agrees. Bad results get published the same way as good ones, or not at all.

How we set the price

Keeping one consultant pays for about three years of Sphyr. By our middle estimate, losing a consultant costs a firm about ₹8.6 lakh in recruiter fees and the months before a replacement is up to speed, and Sphyr for 150 people costs ₹2.68 lakh a year. The ₹8.6 lakh is our estimate of what an exit costs. Nobody has measured whether Sphyr prevents exits.

₹149 is 32% of the lowest list price we found for a dedicated review tool: PerformYard's $5 a seat, about ₹464, for firms of several hundred people. Peoplebox and Confirm start at about ₹743, so we are about a fifth of theirs. We read these prices on 24 November 2026.

What happens after you sign

Your first cards go out on a date you agree with us. Here is what each person gets, and when.

Before the first cycle
In a short kickoff with our team, your head of talent adds the firm's own values, and those words become the tags on every card. Together you pick the day the first cycle opens.
Also before it opens
We show your partners a sample record built from people on four engagements, then the same screen for their own practice, still empty. It starts filling from the date you picked, and the decisions stay with them.
The week before
Each consultant gets a note from the managing partner and adds the people they work with to their circle.
The first cycle
One card per person in your circle, about two minutes in all. While you fill in theirs, they are filling in yours.
After a few cycles
Consultants start to see what their colleagues noticed, along with the notes written to them. Engagement managers see how the current team is landing while the work is still going on, and your head of talent sees who took part, team by team, without chasing anyone.
Every two weeks after that
A new set of cards. Over time each person builds up a line showing how their work is landing.
