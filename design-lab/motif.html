Ribbon motif

The ribbons are the people who saw the work. Strands use the cool logo colours, picked at gate 1b.

The core visual

For the home page hero. Each engagement brings a few people (strands). The engagement ends and fades to an outline; its strands stay in the record, and at the promotion round the record is the sphere. It plays once on load; with reduced motion it shows the finished picture. Hover or focus the sphere to see the ribbons part.

The circle

For "How it works". Fourteen strands for fourteen people, the average circle in the 2018 paper-and-pen pilot run by Sphyr's founder. Every strand turns the same way, like the ribbons in the logo. Pick a group to see its people. The strands draw in as the section scrolls into view.

Logo variants

Every variant keeps the sphere and its 15 separate ribbons. One-colour variants draw a thin gap in the page colour around each ribbon so they stay apart. Shown at 120, 28 and 16 pixels.
