Sphyr
Components Faux UI

Product views

Real screens from the Sphyr prototype, rebuilt in HTML and cropped to the part that carries the point. Layout, labels and microcopy follow the prototype. People, roles and team names are neutral placeholders until the persona copy is final.

Giving feedback

For the home page hero or the walkthrough. The rater's card from the prototype's Provide Feedback screen, with the "Private by design" card from its right rail as the one satellite. Pick an answer to see the next step change, as it does in the product.

What the person rated sees

For how-it-works. The prototype's member Insights screen: three of its four score tiles and the trajectory over six fortnightly cycles, with one anonymized comment as the satellite. The window fades at its foot over the chart, never over words.

What a manager sees

For the home page or how-it-works. The prototype's Team Insights screen: team score and participation, then standouts and members needing support. No satellite here: any card over this window would cover a name or a score.

Team against the organization

For how-it-works, beside the manager view. The prototype's benchmark card, whole: the team average against the organization average on the seven dimensions.

Edit approvals, shown honestly

For the trust page. The whole card, not a crop, because it shows the one place where a manager sees who gave feedback to whom. The caption says so in plain words.

Compact designs at 358px

Each frame has a second composition for a phone column, switched by a container query under 440 or 600px. Satellites move or drop; words stay whole.

Sphyr design lab. Course project, Product Marketing (PD2.501), IIIT Hyderabad. Product views rebuilt from the Sphyr prototype at sphyr-prototype.raviwarrier.com with placeholder sample data.
