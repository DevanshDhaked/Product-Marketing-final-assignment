Trust

Honest feedback needs real anonymity.

People only rate honestly if they're sure it can't come back to them. This page explains exactly how Sphyr protects that, in plain language.

Can my manager see who wrote what? No.

Three rules that protect reviewers

Rule 1

Pool before showing

Ratings are combined and anonymized before anyone sees them. Nobody sees a single person's rating.

Rule 2

Wait for enough reviewers

Results stay hidden until enough reviewers have responded. If only one or two people answered, nothing appears.

Rule 3

Release notes in batches

Written notes can be delayed and released together, so "she wrote that the day after our meeting" isn't possible.

Feedback, not surveillance

Sphyr only knows what people choose to tell it on a card. It doesn't track activity, read email, log keystrokes or monitor screens. The only data is ratings, tags and optional notes that colleagues give each other.

Sphyr doesn't decide promotions either. Managers and founders still make the call. They just have more than one view to go on.

Does collusion work?

People sometimes worry that friends will rate each other up. In the 2018 pen-and-paper pilot of the method (four companies, about 14 weeks), the tendency to collude dropped to 0% after three weeks. Pooling across a whole Sphere, every fortnight, makes a few friendly ratings hard to notice.

This result is for the method, not for today's software.

Your data

What we collect

Employee names, roles and reporting lines, imported from your CSV or XLSX file. Then the ratings, tags and notes people give on their cards. Clients and vendors invited to a Sphere give only their name and email.

What we use it for

To run feedback cycles and show pooled results to the right people. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Your HR system

Sphyr runs alongside it and doesn't write to it. Import is by file today. Direct HRMS connectors are planned.

Refunds and leaving

Annual plan: full refund if your first six fortnightly cycles (about 12 weeks) show you nothing new. Renewal terms are stated upfront, with a reminder 30 days before renewal.

Course project note: hosting location, encryption standards and certifications aren't specified in Sphyr's source documents, so we don't claim any here.
