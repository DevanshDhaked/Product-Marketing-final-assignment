Why ₹149

How we arrived at the price

We published how the price was set, so you can check it.

1 · Our costs: about ₹30 per employee

Infrastructure, support and setup work out to roughly ₹30 per employee per month. At an 80% target margin, that sets a floor of about ₹150. ₹149 sits on that floor.

2 · Your value: about ₹667 per employee per month

We estimated this from two things: lower attrition, using published attrition-cost data, and less time spent preparing reviews, using the time-cost method in the Sphyr whitepaper. At ₹149 you keep most of that value.

3 · The market: performance tools usually cost much more

For example, Lattice lists its Performance product at $10 per seat per month (about ₹930 at ₹93/USD), checked on lattice.com/pricing on 24 November 2026. Full HR suites sell performance as one module in a bigger bundle. Sphyr does one job and is priced for it.

Why one flat rate and not tiers?

With tiered pricing, hiring the 101st person can raise the rate for the first 100. That punishes growth, and growing companies are exactly who we serve. A flat rate means your bill grows only in line with your headcount.
