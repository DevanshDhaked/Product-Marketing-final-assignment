The 2-minute walkthrough

You pick a consultant to promote and fill in one card for a colleague. Then you see the record partners would have open when they decide, with no sign-up needed.

Sample data, fictional people. Tara and Nikhil are an example, not real consultants. Nothing you choose or type here leaves this page.

Now fill in one card the way Nikhil's colleagues do

Every two weeks, each person in a consultant's circle fills in a short card about them. Imagine you were on Nikhil's engagement team this fortnight.

What partners see for both of them

This is Sphyr's Member Insights view after six cycles of cards, about 12 weeks, from the 14 people in each consultant's circle. Nikhil's cards came from 13 of the 14 people in that circle, against 10 for Tara. The tags they picked most for Nikhil were Takes Initiative and Solution-oriented.

The engagement ends. The evidence stays.

Partners still decide. Sphyr gives them the view of the people who saw the work, cycle by cycle, while the engagement team is still together.

In a firm that uses Sphyr, a record like this builds up for every consultant, two minutes at a time.
