Propaganda strategy

This is our plan to change one belief held by heads of talent at homegrown consulting firms. Deliverable 2 of 4.

The engagement ends. The evidence stays.

Audience
Heads of talent at homegrown Indian consulting firms of 40 to 400 people, and their managing partners.
Expected response
Take the 2-minute walkthrough, then propose a 12-week paid pilot to the managing partner.
Belief today
“Nobody remembers a consultant’s whole year. That is how consulting works.”
Belief we want
“The record breaks up with every engagement team, and we can fix that.”
Tactics
Name the problem and blame rotation, never partners.
Channels
Free channels led by the Sphyr LinkedIn page, plus one paid standee.

Audience

We write for the head of talent at a homegrown Indian consulting firm of 40 to 400 people. Below about 40 people, partners still see everyone's work, and above about 400, firms buy large HR software. We leave out IT consulting and global firms.

Belief

The belief today was true when partners saw everyone's work. Now people rotate, and the record breaks up with each team. One late rating is weak evidence: in two samples of over 2,000 managers each, rater habits explained more of a rating than performance did (Scullen, Mount and Goff, 2000).

Central message

Engagements end every few weeks, and what the team saw leaves with them. The line blames how work rotates, so partners can hear it without getting defensive. Most people see a post or a standee once, so it has to work in one reading.

Tactics and channels

Every channel is free except one planned standee.

Why they fit

Heads of talent are short of time and wary of vendors, so the price sits on the home page and the walkthrough asks for nothing. A short name gives them words for partners, and blaming rotation accuses no one. One line on every piece links posts seen weeks apart. They trust peers more, so we go through introductions and HR meets.

Link to positioning

Our positioning calls Sphyr 360 feedback in two minutes every two weeks, from the people each consultant works with, so partners decide on the whole year. Rotation is the cause we name. Two minutes a fortnight answers the fear of admin, and partners deciding answers the partner’s veto.

Expected response

The first sign is a head of talent who tells partners the record breaks up with the team.
