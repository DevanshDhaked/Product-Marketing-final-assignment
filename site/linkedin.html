Feedback from the people who saw the work

Week 1 · Public

Four people saw the workshop. None were in calibration.
A senior consultant built the pricing model on a retail project, then ran the client workshop while the engagement manager was out sick. Four people from the firm were in the room, and the client's CFO asked for the deck that night.

…more

Ten months later the project team has rolled off to three other clients. HR has one feedback form, written by the engagement manager from memory of the last few weeks, and the consultant's self-review covers the rest.

In calibration, the partner the consultant is now staffed with speaks for them. That partner never saw the workshop, and nobody calls the four. The room moves to the next name, and the designation and the hike wait another cycle.

Partners and heads of talent: before calibration, how do you reach the people who saw a consultant's best project, and what do you ask them?

Feedback from the people who saw the work

Week 3 · Public

One manager writes the rating for four projects.
That manager saw one of the four, usually the last one, and mostly its final weeks. The other three reach the rating secondhand.

…more

Research on performance ratings finds that more than half of a rating reflects the person who gave it. A quarter or less reflects the work itself.

So every two weeks, Sphyr asks everyone on the project to rate the colleagues they worked with, in about two minutes. By calibration, partners and engagement managers see grouped ratings from everyone on a consultant's projects across the year, with comments anonymized to the person rated.

Rate one sample colleague yourself in the 2-minute walkthrough. The link is in the first comment.

Feedback from the people who saw the work

Week 5 · Public

12 weeks in, how will you know the pilot worked?
Scope it like a client project. Write these four checks down before anyone signs:

…more
  1. The line that means it failed, and who calls it.
  2. Who still takes part after the second cycle. If people drop off, the tool is extra work.
  3. One question for partners at the end: what did you learn about a consultant that you did not know? Ask for a name.
  4. One question for consultants: would you keep doing this if HR stopped chasing you?

Our pilot is built on these checks, with one practice of up to 40 people for 12 weeks. The firm sets each target before the first cycle and keeps the results.

Book a 20-minute call to plan one before your next appraisal cycle. The link is in the first comment.

How the three work together

The team reads every answer to the first post before the second goes out, and replies to every comment within the first day. People on our team reshare each post with a sentence or two in their own words. The standee ad runs at an HR event in the same weeks as the first post.
