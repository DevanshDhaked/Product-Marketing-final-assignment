How we set the price

The pilot fee covers what a pilot costs us to run: about 30 hours of our team's time plus 12 weeks of running costs. At the plan rate, the same pilot would bring in about ₹16,500, less than it costs.

  • ₹149 is about a third of the cheapest standalone appraisal software we found. That is PerformYard, whose published price for firms of a few hundred people is about ₹464 per employee per month.
  • At this rate, about 49 firms of 150 people cover our fixed costs.
  • We think six fortnightly cycles is about the fewest that show partners a pattern they can use, so the pilot runs 12 weeks.
  • A price survey of heads of talent and partners will decide whether ₹149 changes. A change would apply only to firms that sign after it.

What happens after a firm signs

The first feedback goes out on a date the firm agrees with us.

Before the first cycle
In a short kickoff with our team, the head of talent adds the firm's own values, which become the feedback tags. The head of talent and our team pick the day the first cycle opens.
Also before it opens
We show the partners a sample Member Insights view built from people on four projects, then the same screen for their own practice, still empty. The decisions stay with the partners.
The week before
The managing partner sends each consultant a note. Each consultant then adds the people they work with closely to their circle.
The first cycle
Each consultant gives feedback to each person in their circle, about two minutes in all, while those people give feedback on them.
After a few cycles
Consultants see what colleagues noticed, with the comments written to them. Engagement managers see how the project team is landing while the work is still going on. The head of talent sees who took part without chasing anyone.
Every two weeks after that
A new round of feedback opens, and over time each consultant's Insights shows a line of how their work is landing.
After the first year
Our team goes through the year with the firm, including voluntary exits against the year before if the firm agrees. The firm decides on renewal from its own results.
