Deliverables

Each of the four launch deliverables has its own page. All four repeat “The engagement ends. The evidence stays.” and follow one buyer journey, mapped in the campaign flow.

  1. 01

    The website

    The home page and the 2-minute walkthrough show a head of talent what Sphyr does and what a pilot costs.

  2. 02

    Propaganda strategy

    How we plan to change what heads of talent and managing partners believe about the record a consultant leaves behind.

  3. 03

    Three LinkedIn posts

    Posts for the Sphyr company page, one for each early stage of the journey, from naming the problem to weighing a pilot.

  4. 04

    The advertisement

    A standee with handout cards, planned for an HR community event, that reaches heads of talent among their peers while they are first naming the problem.
