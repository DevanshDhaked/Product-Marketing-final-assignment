Rationale

MediumLinkedIn Sponsored Content, single image, 1:1. Founders aren't searching for a fix to a problem they don't see yet, so search ads miss them. LinkedIn lets us target by role and company size, and the format sits in the same feed as our organic posts.
AudienceJob titles: Founder, Co-founder, CEO, Head of HR/People. Company size: 51–200 and 201–500. India, IT services and software. Retargeting: site visitors and people who engaged with Posts 1–2.
MessageOne promise: Promote on merit, not politics. The proof line covers the three hard-to-copy words: two minutes, anonymous, everyone.
BenefitFairer promotions from evidence, at almost no time cost to employees.
VisualDark background stands out in a white feed. The one-dot vs. ring contrast gets the idea across even if nobody reads the copy. It's the same image as Post 2 and the home page, so each exposure builds on the last.
CTA"See the pooled view in 2 minutes" leads to the walkthrough, not "Book a demo". Our persona avoids sales calls. A low-commitment step suits a cold audience.
Pricing consistencyShows the real price and billing terms (₹149, billed annually), exactly as on the pricing page. Showing the price early filters out poor-fit buyers and signals openness.
Journey stageAwareness → interest. Runs from week 3, alongside Post 2 (see the campaign sequence).
Test and measureA/B test the headline "merit, not politics" against the fallback "merit, not memory". Track click-through rate, walkthrough starts and walkthrough completions.
